Hisory

My childhood was filled with a love for animals, curiosity in the kitchen, and an early interest in drawing and painting. These were the things I spent most of my time enjoying as I grew up.

Animals

I’ve always wanted to have a pet, but I was never allowed to. Luckily, that did not prevent me from having a big interest in animals. I was especially fond of cats and horses. My mother got me a horse whip and a riding helmet once for my birthday, even if we could not afford riding school. However, I could still be close to animals by going to after school activities at city farms. My interest in animals was truly all encompassing, my dream was to become a veterinarian.

Top Favourite Animals

  1. Deers
  2. Cats
  3. Horses
  4. Dolphines

Baking and Cooking

Most children enjoy sweets and like experimenting by mixing different ingredients, or substances, into more or less edible concoctions. This is how my interest in baking and cooking originated. It started with small pastries like chocolate balls, scones and oatmeal cookies. Later, I was frequently baking and cooking all kinds of different dishes. Pizza was one of my favourite things to make and is still a dish I make regularly today.

Painting

I started drawing and painting very young. I soon became very fond of using watercolor paints to paint pictures of different objects. My mother enlisted me to a painting school where I could learn more about painting and different techniques that could be used. She also gifted me a beautiful watercolor paint set that I still use today.
