Future

In the future, I hope to explore creative hobbies more deeply, such as knitting, crocheting, and photography. I look forward to learning new techniques, taking on bigger projects, and improving the quality of the work I create.

Knitting

Knitting is a very relaxing activity that can be very diverse. I’ve only knitted small simple things in the past, but I would like to start bigger projects. Some of the things I can see myself learning how to knit are different garments such as cardigans, jumpers, socks and gloves. Knitting also seems like a great side activity. Once you get good at knitting, you can do other things in parallel, such as listening to podcasts or watching movies.

Crochet

Like knitting, crocheting is also a very relaxing activity with great opportunities for creativity. The two are similar indeed, often, something one can knit, it is also possible to achieve something similar by crochet. The crafts are, however, suited for different kinds of projects. I would like to explore crochet more and all the different crafts one can make with it.

Photography

Currently, I only take pictures with my phone, but in the future, I would love to own a professional camera. This would allow me to achieve high quality pictures that are impossible to achieve, even with the most expensive phone! Things I would like to take pictures of include food, nature and memories that are worth capturing.
